FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this TCPA compliant?

Yes. Inbound submissions require an explicit, unchecked consent checkbox covering automated, pre-recorded, and AI-generated voice calls and texts. LeadPilot identifies as Postema Media Management, honors opt-outs (text STOP, a verbal request, or email), and live dialing stays behind standard KYC verification.

Does it do outbound calling, or just callbacks?

Both. LeadPilot works your lists automatically on the outbound side, and rings new leads back in about eight seconds on the inbound side. Same AI caller, same booking flow, one pipeline.

How natural does the AI sound?

It holds a real, concise conversation and handles back-and-forth. Most prospects don't realize it's AI until they're told. You can customize the voice, tone and script on Growth and Pro.

Does it work with my CRM?

It's built to plug straight into GoHighLevel via an inbound webhook — creating the contact and triggering the Voice AI outbound call automatically. Other CRMs wire in the same way.

What happens if a lead doesn't answer?

They get a friendly text-back so the conversation stays alive, and the miss is logged in your CRM for follow-up.
