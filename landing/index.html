FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it sound like a robot?

No. LeadPilot uses real-time conversational voice that listens and responds naturally, handles interruptions, and adapts to the conversation. Most people don't realize it isn't a person until you tell them.

Can it use my own phone numbers?

Yes. It calls from your existing numbers so caller ID stays consistent with your brand, and it respects your calling hours.

What happens when someone's interested?

The AI books them onto your live calendar during the call, writes the appointment and a full transcript back to your CRM, and can hand off to a human any time the prospect asks.

Is it compliant?

LeadPilot is built for B2B calling with do-not-call suppression, calling-hour limits, and clear identification on every call. You control the list and the script; we give you the guardrails to stay on the right side of the rules.

How fast can we go live?

Most teams are running their first campaign within days — load a list, set your script and calendar, and start dialing.
